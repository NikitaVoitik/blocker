FAQ

Can I really not bypass the block?

You can technically remove a site from the blocklist — but the extension tracks that too. The point isn't to make it impossible to access sites, it's to make you feel bad enough about it that you stop. The shame selfie alone is usually enough.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

Absolutely not. All photos are stored using Chrome's local storage API. They never leave your browser. There is no server, no cloud, no sync. Uninstall the extension and they're gone forever.

What if I don't want the camera feature?

Camera permission is optional. If you deny it, the extension still blocks sites and shows shame messages — you just won't get the selfie capture. The blocking and time tracking work independently.

Does it work in Incognito mode?

Yes — if you enable the extension in Incognito mode through Chrome's extension settings. We see you trying to sneak around.

What sites come pre-blocked?

Twitter/X and YouTube Shorts are blocked by default. You can add any website you want — Reddit, TikTok, Instagram, or anything else that's destroying your productivity. The tracker tab comes pre-loaded with the top 10 social media time-wasters.

What are the "shame levels"?

Your shame level escalates based on total attempts: Rookie (0–9), Repeat Offender (10–49), Addict (50–99), Terminal Brain Rot (100–499), and Beyond Saving (500+). Each level unlocks progressively more intense visual effects and roasts.

Is it free?

Yes. Completely free. No premium tier. No ads. No in-app purchases. Just pure, unfiltered shame.
