Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

Overview

Punishment Mode is a Chrome extension that blocks distracting websites and tracks browsing attempts locally. This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any user data whatsoever.

Data Storage

All data generated by the extension is stored locally on your device using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.local API. This includes:

  • Your list of blocked and tracked websites
  • Attempt counts and timestamps
  • Webcam photos captured on the block page
  • Time tracking statistics

None of this data ever leaves your browser. There is no server, no cloud storage, no sync service, and no external database.

Webcam Usage

The extension uses your device's camera solely to capture a photo when you visit a blocked site. These photos are stored locally in your browser's extension storage. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any external service or third party.

Camera access requires your explicit permission and can be revoked at any time through Chrome's site settings.

Permissions

  • declarativeNetRequest / declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess — Required to redirect blocked sites to the extension's block page. Broad host access is necessary because users can block any website.
  • storage — Required to save settings, blocked site list, attempt history, and captured photos locally.
  • offscreen — Required to access the camera for photo capture in the background.
  • webNavigation — Required as a backup mechanism to detect navigation to blocked sites.
  • alarms — Required for periodic time tracking updates.

Data Collection

This extension does not collect any data. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No browsing history is collected or transmitted
  • No analytics or tracking scripts are included
  • No data is sent to any server or third party
  • No cookies are set or read by the extension
  • No user accounts are created or required

Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs. The only external resource loaded is Google Fonts for display purposes.

Data Deletion

All extension data can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing the extension's storage through Chrome's settings. Since no data is stored externally, uninstalling the extension permanently removes all associated data.

Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this privacy policy will be reflected in the extension's update notes and this document.
